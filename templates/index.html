What This App Does

The model uses features like reading score, writing score, gender, race or ethnicity, lunch type, parental education, and test preparation course to help predict student math performance.

  • Loads student data and prepares training and test sets
  • Applies preprocessing for numeric and categorical features
  • Trains multiple regression models and selects the strongest one
